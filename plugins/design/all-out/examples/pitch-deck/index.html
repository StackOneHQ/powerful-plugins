Where the data comes from

The tide is just rhythms, added up.

Every port publishes harmonic constants: the size and timing of each pull from the moon and sun. We sum them for each harbour, then correct the result with public weather models, every hour.

Why it compounds Illustrative

Every harbour gets sharper the longer it runs.

Local gauge readings and skipper reports feed back into each harbour's own correction: its sill, its silt, its swell. Forecast error at the sill falls from 14 cm in week one to 4 cm by week twelve.
